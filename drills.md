# Home drills

Short drills to fix my mistakes at home, before they become habits. One drill per mistake.
Pi Liang adds a drill whenever the coach corrects something; I mark it **Fixed** when the coach stops correcting it.

Status: **Active** (practise it) or **Fixed** (keep for reference).

## Wall pivot kick drill
- **Fixes:** I lose my balance on the roundhouse kick when I turn my hips.
- **Coach's cue:** None yet - from my own notes. Coach's kick cues: turn the hips over, back straight or leaning slightly to the side, don't lean back.
- **Technique:** Rear roundhouse kick, switch kick
- **From:** Day 3 (2026-10-14)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 10 slow kicks each leg.
- **How:**
  1. Stand in stance next to a wall or chair, the hand on the non-kicking side resting on it.
  2. Rise onto the ball of the standing foot and turn its heel towards the target.
  3. Turn the hips over and swing the shin through slowly at calf height. Don't lean back.
  4. Hold the finish for 2 seconds, then return to stance.
  5. When it feels steady, take the hand off the wall and kick at half speed.

## Elbow wall drill
- **Fixes:** My elbow swings out to the side before the jab and cross.
- **Coach's cue:** Drive the elbow forward, not out to the side. Arm straight, fist slightly tilted.
- **Technique:** 1 - Jab, 2 - Cross
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 10 slow jabs, then 3 x 10 slow crosses. About 3 minutes.
- **How:**
  1. Stand in stance, side-on to a wall, lead shoulder about a fist's width from it.
  2. Throw a slow jab. The elbow should slide straight forward, just brushing past the wall.
  3. If the elbow bumps the wall, it flared. Reset and go slower.
  4. Turn around so the rear side faces the wall and do the same with the cross.
  5. Finish each punch with the fist slightly tilted, not fully palm-down.

## Loose shoulders shadowboxing
- **Fixes:** I'm too stiff - hands, guard, shoulders and neck - so my muscles tire fast.
- **Coach's cue:** Relax. Too stiff.
- **Technique:** Guard, all punches
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 2 rounds of 2 minutes, 30 seconds rest.
- **How:**
  1. Shadowbox at half speed in front of a mirror.
  2. Every few punches, check: shoulders down, away from the ears.
  3. Breathe out on every punch. Tighten the fist only at the moment it lands, then loosen.
  4. Between rounds, shake out the arms and roll the shoulders back 5 times.

## Uppercut mirror drill
- **Fixes:** My forearm tilts forward on the uppercut instead of staying vertical.
- **Coach's cue:** Elbow to fist straight up and down. Fist ends about a hand span from my face. Knees bend, shoulder dips, then punch low to high.
- **Technique:** 5 - Lead uppercut, 6 - Rear uppercut
- **From:** Day 1 (2026-10-09)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 8 each side, slow.
- **How:**
  1. Stand side-on to a mirror so you can see your arm from the side.
  2. Small dip, then drive up from the legs.
  3. Freeze at the top: forearm vertical, fist a hand span from your face, palm facing you.
  4. If the forearm leans forward, reset and go slower.

## Glued guard check
- **Fixes:** In sparring, partners' punches knock my own gloves into my face.
- **Coach's cue:** None yet - from my own sparring notes.
- **Technique:** Guard
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 rounds of 1 minute.
- **How:**
  1. Shadowbox lightly. Every 10 seconds, freeze.
  2. Check: both gloves resting on the forehead or cheekbones, elbows close to the ribs, chin tucked.
  3. Gentle contact, not squeezing - stay relaxed while the guard stays home.
  4. After every punch, the hand comes straight back to the same spot.

## Combo shadowboxing
- **Fixes:** In sparring I throw one punch, pause, then the next, instead of combos.
- **Coach's cue:** Practise combos like 1-2 and 1-2-3.
- **Technique:** 1-2 (jab-cross), 1-2-3 (jab-cross-lead hook)
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 rounds of 2 minutes, 30 seconds rest.
- **How:**
  1. Round 1: only 1-2. Say "one-two" as one word and throw it as one movement, then step back to guard.
  2. Round 2: 1-2-3. Let the cross turn your hips so the hook comes straight after, no pause.
  3. Round 3: mix them - 1-2, then 1-2-3, then 1-1-2.
  4. Stay relaxed: shoulders down, breathe out on each punch ("tss-tss").
