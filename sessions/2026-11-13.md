## Lesson

### Cross to the body (2 to the body)
**From class**
- Punch number 2 (the **cross**), but to the body instead of the head.

**How it works** *(added by Pi Liang)*
- Get to the body by bending the knees and dropping your level, not by bending forward at the waist or punching downward. Your fist travels roughly straight, at the height of their stomach or solar plexus.
- Same mechanics as the head cross: pivot the rear foot, turn the hips, elbow behind the fist, land on the first two knuckles.
- The lead hand stays glued to your face and your rear shoulder comes up to the chin - when you drop to the body, your head is closer to their hands.
- Common beginner mistakes: leaning the head out past the front knee, dropping the eyes to the floor, and staying low after the punch. Punch, then come back up to your stance.
- Home: shadowbox 2 to the head, then 2 to the body, 10 of each pair - check in a mirror that your back stays fairly straight and your eyes stay up.

### Duck under the punch, then counter cross
**From class**
- Practise defending by **ducking** [lower the leg and body]: the opponent throws punches, and I bend my legs and lower my body so the punch goes over me.
- When I lower my body, my eyes stay on the opponent's collar.
- Then I come back up to stance and throw the **cross** (punch number 2).

**How it works** *(added by Pi Liang)*
- A duck is a level change: bend the knees and sink straight down, keeping the back fairly straight. It's different from a slip (Day 12), which moves the head to the side.
- Drop only as far as you need for the punch to pass over your head. Beginners often go too deep, which is slow to come back from and puts the head near their knee.
- Eyes up the whole time - your coach's cue to watch the collar matches the guard cue from Day 9 (eyes on the base of the neck). Gloves stay on the face while you're down there.
- Coming up loads the punch: push up from the legs and turn the hips into the cross, so the leg drive goes into it. That's why the counter can be strong.
- The weight you felt in your thighs is expected - the quads hold you in the low position and then drive you back up.
- Home: in front of a mirror, 10 slow reps of duck - up - cross. Hold the low position for one second each time and check: back straight, gloves on the face, eyes level.
