# Basic combos

Common beginner punch combos for the Combo caller. Pi Liang keeps this list; combos my coach teaches go in `techniques.md` (Combos table) and show up as "My combos".

Punch numbers: 1 jab - 2 cross - 3 lead hook - 4 rear hook - 5 lead uppercut - 6 rear uppercut

| Combo | Punches | Range |
|---|---|---|
| 1-1 | jab, jab | long |
| 1-2 | jab, cross | long |
| 1-1-2 | jab, jab, cross | long |
| 1-2-3 | jab, cross, lead hook | mid |
| 1-2-3-2 | jab, cross, lead hook, cross | mid |
| 3-2 | lead hook, cross | close |
| 2-3-2 | cross, lead hook, cross | mid |
| 1-2-5-2 | jab, cross, lead uppercut, cross | mid |
| 2-5-2 | cross, lead uppercut, cross | close |
| 5-4 | lead uppercut, rear hook | close |
| 6-3-2 | rear uppercut, lead hook, cross | close |
| 1-6-3-2 | jab, rear uppercut, lead hook, cross | mid |
