## Words to know
- **Nak Muay** - Muay Thai practitioner
- **Kru** - teacher / coach
- **Teep** - push kick
- **Khao** - knee
- **Sok** - elbow
- **Wai Kru Ram Muay** - pre-fight ritual dance honouring teachers
