# Home drills

Short drills to fix my mistakes at home, before they become habits. One drill per mistake.
Pi Liang adds a drill whenever the coach corrects something; I mark it **Fixed** when the coach stops correcting it.

Status: **Active** (practise it) or **Fixed** (keep for reference).

## Morning review flow (12-15 min)
- **Fixes:** Only 10-15 minutes in the morning to review everything I've learned. Moves are chained together here, the way they happen in class and sparring, instead of practised one at a time.
- **Coach's cue:** None - Pi Liang's plan, built from what the coach taught on Days 1-4.
- **Technique:** Guard, 1-2 (jab-cross), 1-2-3 (jab-cross-lead hook), 1-2-1-2 (double one-two), Defence vs 1-2 (straights), Defence vs 3-4 (hooks), Defence vs 5-6 (uppercuts), Checking kicks (shin block), Rear roundhouse kick (right), Lead roundhouse kick - switch kick (left), Catch and sweep
- **From:** Day 4 (2026-10-16)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 6 blocks, about 12 minutes, plus 15 seconds' rest between blocks. Half speed, no power - clean form beats speed. Short on time? Do blocks 2, 3 and 4 only (7 minutes).
- **How:**
  1. **Warm-up - 1 min.** Rope-less skipping: small bounces on the balls of the feet, wrists turning small circles. Then roll the shoulders back 5 times.
  2. **Hands - 2 min.** In stance, throw 1-2, then 1-2-3, then 1-2-1-2, and repeat (about 30 combos). Use the Combo caller if you like. After every combo: hands back to the glued guard, rear foot pivots on every cross, breathe out on each punch.
  3. **Defend, then answer - 2 min.** Imagine a partner. Block the straights -> answer 1-2. Cover the left hook, then the right hook -> answer 1-2-3. Close up for the uppercut -> answer 1-2. Eyes clear above the gloves, chin tucked. About 10 rounds of the three.
  4. **Check, then kick - 2 min.** Check with the left leg -> right kick at calf height. Check with the right leg -> switch kick with the left. Then 1-2 -> right kick. Slow: rise onto the ball of the standing foot, turn the hips over, land with the shin, foot pointed. About 10 of each chain.
  5. **Catch and sweep - 2 min.** Walk through it in the air, 5 times each side, saying it out loud: "catch right, step back, step forward, sweep left, push left" - then the mirror. After each sweep, back to guard and finish with a 1-2.
  6. **Reflexes - 2 min.** Wall dot ball catch (below). No ball? Use the Combo caller and fire each call as fast as you can with good form.
  7. **Cool-down - 1 min.** Hip flexor lunge, 30 seconds each side.
  8. When the coach teaches something new, swap it into the matching block rather than adding a new one.

## Hip-turn kick drill
- **Fixes:** I forget to turn my hips when I kick because I'm focused on footwork and the target; my left (switch) kick is weaker and the switch isn't smooth.
- **Coach's cue:** Use the hips - turn the hips over when you kick. After 1-2-3, step the lead foot out to the left a little, then kick to the body.
- **Technique:** Rear roundhouse kick (right), Lead roundhouse kick - switch kick (left), 1-2-3 + right body kick
- **From:** Day 6 (2026-10-26)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 rounds of 1 minute, 30 seconds rest. Slow, no power.
- **How:**
  1. Round 1 - right kick, hips only: in stance, kick slowly at waist height. Think only "belly button turns to the side, standing heel to the target". Ignore the target.
  2. Round 2 - switch kick: small hop, then kick straight away in one beat - no landing and stopping. Same hip cue. Hands up during the hop.
  3. Round 3 - combo: 1-2-3, small step out to the left with the lead foot, right kick to the body. Back to guard.
  4. Hand on a wall or chair if you lose balance; take it away when it feels steady.

## Side-step box drill
- **Fixes:** In tag and sparring I keep circling instead of moving left and right, and it drains my stamina.
- **Coach's cue:** None yet - from my own notes.
- **Technique:** Footwork - forward / back / left / right
- **From:** Day 5 (2026-10-19)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 rounds of 1 minute, 30 seconds rest.
- **How:**
  1. In stance, guard up, on the balls of the feet. Imagine a small square on the floor.
  2. Step forward, step left, step back, step right - one small step each, then reset your stance.
  3. Lead with the foot closest to where you're going: left foot first to go left, right foot first to go right. Feet never cross.
  4. Round 2: add a 1-2 after every side-step.
  5. Round 3: free - mix side-steps, steps in and steps out. Count how often you circle; aim for less.

## Punch and pivot
- **Fixes:** In sparring I keep circling instead of attacking and getting out at an angle; I stay in front of my partner after I punch.
- **Coach's cue:** None - from a video I saved ("3 footwork drills to attack + exit safely", Drill 3).
- **Technique:** Footwork - forward / back / left / right, 1-2 (jab-cross), 1-2-3 (jab-cross-lead hook)
- **From:** Day 5 (2026-10-19)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 rounds of 1 minute, 30 seconds rest.
- **How:**
  1. In stance, guard up. Throw a 1-2 at an imaginary partner.
  2. Pivot: stay on the ball of the lead foot and swing the rear foot around about a quarter turn, so you end up facing the side of where your partner was.
  3. Reset your stance and guard, then throw the next 1-2 from the new angle.
  4. Round 2: 1-2-3, then pivot. Round 3: mix in the step in and out - 1-2, step back out of range.
  5. Small, quick pivots. Hands stay up while you turn.

## Check and cover drill
- **Fixes:** When I check a kick, I forget to cover my face - partners kick to make me check, then punch me.
- **Coach's cue:** None - from a teammate's tip in sparring.
- **Technique:** Checking kicks (shin block), Guard
- **From:** Day 5 (2026-10-19)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 10 checks, alternating legs.
- **How:**
  1. Stand in stance in front of a mirror, guard up.
  2. Lift the lead leg to check: same-side elbow drops to meet the knee, the other glove stays on your cheek.
  3. Freeze for 1 second and look: is the face still covered? Chin tucked?
  4. Put the foot down and answer straight away with a 1-2.
  5. Switch to the rear leg and repeat.

## Wall dot ball catch
- **Fixes:** Slow reactions; seeing strikes coming from outside where I'm looking.
- **Coach's cue:** None - from a video I watched. Trains peripheral vision: in a fight your eyes stay on the opponent's chest or base of the neck, so punches and kicks come from the corner of your eye.
- **Technique:** Guard
- **From:** Day 4 (2026-10-16)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 30 seconds, 15 seconds rest. About 2 minutes.
- **How:**
  1. Mark a small dot on the wall at eye level. Stand about 1.5-2 m away with a tennis ball.
  2. Keep your eyes on the dot the whole time - don't look at the ball.
  3. Throw the ball at the wall with one hand and catch it with the other hand.
  4. Switch: throw with the other hand and catch with the first.
  5. Getting easy? Step closer, throw a little harder, or stand in your fighting stance with the guard up between throws.

## Ball drop catch
- **Fixes:** Slow reactions.
- **Coach's cue:** None - from Pi Liang's research.
- **Technique:** Guard
- **From:** Day 4 (2026-10-16)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 2 x 10 catches each hand.
- **How:**
  1. Stand in stance, guard up, a tennis ball in one hand at shoulder height.
  2. Let it drop, then catch it before it bounces a second time.
  3. Hand straight back to guard after every catch.
  4. Harder: drop from lower, or catch before the first bounce.

## Bounce and jab
- **Fixes:** Slow reactions; timing punches to a moving target.
- **Coach's cue:** None - from Pi Liang's research.
- **Technique:** 1 - Jab, 2 - Cross
- **From:** Day 4 (2026-10-16)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 2 x 1 minute.
- **How:**
  1. In stance, bounce the tennis ball on the floor in front of you.
  2. As it rises, throw a light jab at it (touch, not a hard punch), then catch it.
  3. When that's easy: jab-cross at the rising ball, then catch.
  4. Guard stays up between bounces.

## Shin, not foot - bag distance drill
- **Fixes:** I hit the bag with the top of my right foot instead of my shin, and got blisters.
- **Coach's cue:** Kick with the shin, not the foot (Day 3).
- **Technique:** Rear roundhouse kick, body kick
- **From:** Day 4 (2026-10-16)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 8 slow kicks each leg, no power.
- **How:**
  1. Stand in stance facing a wall or a door frame (or a pillow held against the wall).
  2. Raise the leg slowly into the kick and touch the target with the shin, a hand's width above the ankle.
  3. If the foot touches first, you're too far - take a small step in and try again.
  4. Remember that distance; on the bag, step in to it before you kick.
  5. Pivot on the ball of the standing foot every time, heel lifted.

## Wall pivot kick drill
- **Fixes:** I lose my balance on the roundhouse kick when I turn my hips.
- **Coach's cue:** None yet - from my own notes. Coach's kick cues: turn the hips over, back straight or leaning slightly to the side, don't lean back.
- **Technique:** Rear roundhouse kick, switch kick
- **From:** Day 3 (2026-10-14)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 10 slow kicks each leg.
- **How:**
  1. Stand in stance next to a wall or chair, the hand on the non-kicking side resting on it.
  2. Rise onto the ball of the standing foot and turn its heel towards the target.
  3. Turn the hips over and swing the shin through slowly at calf height. Don't lean back.
  4. Hold the finish for 2 seconds, then return to stance.
  5. When it feels steady, take the hand off the wall and kick at half speed.

## Elbow wall drill
- **Fixes:** My elbow swings out to the side before the jab and cross.
- **Coach's cue:** Drive the elbow forward, not out to the side. Arm straight, fist slightly tilted.
- **Technique:** 1 - Jab, 2 - Cross
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 10 slow jabs, then 3 x 10 slow crosses. About 3 minutes.
- **How:**
  1. Stand in stance, side-on to a wall, lead shoulder about a fist's width from it.
  2. Throw a slow jab. The elbow should slide straight forward, just brushing past the wall.
  3. If the elbow bumps the wall, it flared. Reset and go slower.
  4. Turn around so the rear side faces the wall and do the same with the cross.
  5. Finish each punch with the fist slightly tilted, not fully palm-down.

## Loose shoulders shadowboxing
- **Fixes:** I'm too stiff - hands, guard, shoulders and neck - so my muscles tire fast.
- **Coach's cue:** Relax. Too stiff.
- **Technique:** Guard, all punches
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 2 rounds of 2 minutes, 30 seconds rest.
- **How:**
  1. Shadowbox at half speed in front of a mirror.
  2. Every few punches, check: shoulders down, away from the ears.
  3. Breathe out on every punch. Tighten the fist only at the moment it lands, then loosen.
  4. Between rounds, shake out the arms and roll the shoulders back 5 times.

## Uppercut mirror drill
- **Fixes:** My forearm tilts forward on the uppercut instead of staying vertical.
- **Coach's cue:** Elbow to fist straight up and down. Fist ends about a hand span from my face. Knees bend, shoulder dips, then punch low to high.
- **Technique:** 5 - Lead uppercut, 6 - Rear uppercut
- **From:** Day 1 (2026-10-09)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 x 8 each side, slow.
- **How:**
  1. Stand side-on to a mirror so you can see your arm from the side.
  2. Small dip, then drive up from the legs.
  3. Freeze at the top: forearm vertical, fist a hand span from your face, palm facing you.
  4. If the forearm leans forward, reset and go slower.

## Glued guard check
- **Fixes:** In sparring, partners' punches knock my own gloves into my face.
- **Coach's cue:** None yet - from my own sparring notes.
- **Technique:** Guard
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 rounds of 1 minute.
- **How:**
  1. Shadowbox lightly. Every 10 seconds, freeze.
  2. Check: both gloves resting on the forehead or cheekbones, elbows close to the ribs, chin tucked.
  3. Gentle contact, not squeezing - stay relaxed while the guard stays home.
  4. After every punch, the hand comes straight back to the same spot.

## Combo shadowboxing
- **Fixes:** In sparring I throw one punch, pause, then the next, instead of combos.
- **Coach's cue:** Practise combos like 1-2 and 1-2-3.
- **Technique:** 1-2 (jab-cross), 1-2-3 (jab-cross-lead hook)
- **From:** Day 2 (2026-10-12)
- **Status:** Active
- **Dose:** 3 rounds of 2 minutes, 30 seconds rest.
- **How:**
  1. Round 1: only 1-2. Say "one-two" as one word and throw it as one movement, then step back to guard.
  2. Round 2: 1-2-3. Let the cross turn your hips so the hook comes straight after, no pause.
  3. Round 3: mix them - 1-2, then 1-2-3, then 1-1-2.
  4. Stay relaxed: shoulders down, breathe out on each punch ("tss-tss").
