## Lesson

### Kicks on the heavy bag - three targets, both legs
**From class**
- 10 **low kicks**, 10 **thigh kicks**, 10 **body kicks**.
- Both legs: right and left.

**How it works** *(added by Pi Liang)*
- Same roundhouse kick at every height - step out at an angle, rise onto the ball of the standing foot, turn the hips over, land with the shin.
- Lower targets need less hip lift but the same full hip turn - don't let low kicks become lazy leg swings.
- Check the distance on every rep: if the foot lands instead of the shin, take a small step in.
- Home: Shin, not foot - bag distance drill (drills.md).

### Plank relay - punches with the coach
**From class**
- The whole team holds a **plank** while one person punches with the coach. Then the next person goes, and the team keeps planking.
- About 5 sets.
- Then 100 fast **1-2s** (jab-cross).

**How it works** *(added by Pi Liang)*
- Punching after a plank trains you to keep form when the arms and core are tired - the guard is usually the first thing to drop.
- Fast one-twos: stay loose, the jab comes back as the cross goes out, a short breath out on every punch. Tight shoulders = slow punches.
- On a long set, keep a steady rhythm rather than sprinting the first 30 and fading.
- Home: Loose shoulders shadowboxing (drills.md).

### Station rotation - bag, tire, coach, sparring
**From class**
- One person on the bag, one person jumping on the car tire (**tire jumps**), one person with the coach, two teams sparring.
- Rotate through the stations. 2 sets in total.

**How it works** *(added by Pi Liang)*
- Tire jumps build the springy calves and footwork you use on the balls of the feet. Land softly, knees slightly bent, and keep the bounce small and quick.
- In the sparring station, pick one thing to work on each round (for example: answer every block with a 1-2) instead of trying to do everything.
- Home: Morning review flow, block 3 - defend, then answer (drills.md).

### Continuous power kicks - 10 each leg
**From class**
- 10 continuous **rear roundhouse kicks** (right) with full power, with the coach. Then 10 continuous **switch kicks** (left).
- **My mistake:** on the right kick I **hop on the standing (left) foot** [jump on my left feet].
- **Coach's correction:** the left foot stays on the **ball of the foot** and **pivots** [spin it] as the right leg kicks.
- I fixed it on the spot and did 10 more right kicks correctly.
- The left kick is hard because it's not my **dominant leg** [chân thuận].

**How it works** *(added by Pi Liang)*
- Hopping usually happens when you try to add power with a jump. It takes the standing foot off the floor, so you lose your base - less power, and you land off balance.
- The power comes from the pivot: on the ball of the standing foot, the heel turns towards the target as the hips turn over. The foot turns, it doesn't leave the floor.
- On continuous kicks, let the kicking leg come back down to stance and re-plant before the next kick. Rhythm first, then power.
- The left (switch) kick feeling harder is normal on the non-dominant side - it gets extra home reps, not extra power.
- Home: Standing-foot pivot drill (drills.md).

### Core conditioning - ball drops and bag strikes
**From class**
- Lie on the ground in the crunch position and **brace the core** [tight the core]. The coach drops a heavy **medicine ball** onto the core, from high to low, 5 times. Then 20 crunches. 2 sets.
- Then the coach hits each person's core with a punching bag, 10 times. Then 20 crunches.
- My core was tired - I managed about 10-15 of the 20 crunches.

**How it works** *(added by Pi Liang)*
- Bracing: tighten the abs as if someone is about to punch you, and breathe out sharply just as the ball or bag lands. Don't hold a long breath.
- Keep the chin tucked so the head doesn't snap back to the floor on impact.
- This is classic Muay Thai body conditioning - it trains you to take body shots with a braced core, so a kick to the body doesn't knock the wind out of you.
- Crunches when tired: fewer clean reps beat many half reps. 10-15 now is a starting point, not a failure.

## New words
| What I said | Standard term | Note | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| jump on my left feet (on the right kick) | **hopping on the standing foot** | The standing foot stays on the floor - it pivots, it doesn't jump. | Don't hop - stay on the ball of your foot and pivot. |
| spin it (the left foot) | **pivot** | Turn on the ball of the standing foot so the heel points at the target. | Pivot on the ball, heel to the target. |
| chân thuận | **dominant leg** / **strong side** | The other side is your **weak side**. | Your left is your weak side - give it extra reps. |
| heavy ball | **medicine ball** | | Brace - medicine ball coming. |
| tight the core | **brace your core** | Tighten the abs and breathe out on impact. | Brace your core and breathe out when it lands. |
| throwing each hit separately | **throwing single shots** | The opposite of throwing in **combinations** - strikes that flow one into the next. | Stop throwing single shots - put them together. |
